### 🎓 About Me
I am a Ph.D. student at **[RELER](https://github.com/RELER)** Lab, CCAI, **Zhejiang University**. My research journey is driven by the goal of pushing the boundaries of how machines perceive and generate the 3D/4D world.

### 🔬 Research Interests
- **3D/4D Reconstruction**: Exploring robust methods for static and dynamic scene understanding.
- **Generative Models**: Leveraging diffusion models and GANs for high-quality content creation.
- **3D Gaussian Splatting**: Optimizing real-time neural rendering and representation.
- **Human Avatar**: Creating photorealistic and animatable digital humans.

### 🛠️ Currently Working On
- [ ] Advanced 3D Gaussian Splatting techniques.
- [ ] Dynamic Human Avatar Reconstruction from sparse views.
- [ ] Image/Video Diffusion Models.
